## First-title finale — 2008 Brazilian Grand Prix (final round, 2008 season)

Autódromo José Carlos Pace (Interlagos), São Paulo, Brazil — 2008-11-02, round 18.

- Top three finishers, in order: 1. Felipe Massa (Ferrari), 2. Fernando Alonso (Renault), 3. Kimi Räikkönen (Ferrari)
- Hamilton's finishing position: **5th** (started 4th; +38.907 behind the winner)
- Hamilton's fastest lap in that race: **1:14.159** (lap 31, 4th-fastest of the race)

The 98–97 title-winning margin was sealed with that P5 — enough to beat Massa's home win by a single point.

## Constructors' note — 2020 season (most recent title season)

Championship-winning constructor: **Mercedes**, with **573 points** (13 wins; ahead of Red Bull on 319).
